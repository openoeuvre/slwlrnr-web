## Draft series descriptions

Written from the imagery. **Expected to be rewritten** — they are placeholders
in your register, not your words.

### Broad

Figures surface and dissolve in the same gesture. Broad works the tradition of décollage — the torn poster wall — against classical figure painting, letting the body assemble itself out of layers that were never meant to hold an image. What is legible one moment gives way to paper, paste, and the yellow of an advertisement underneath.

### City 2

No figure, no focal point — only the wall itself. City 2 reads the accumulated strata of a billboard hoarding as landscape: pale blue and blush grounds interrupted by black voids where the most recent layer has been stripped away. The composition is a record of successive removals rather than additions.

### Nova 4

Blossoms at a scale the street was never built for. Nova 4 sets magnified petal forms against sprayed and torn urban ground, so the most delicate subject in painting sits on the most abraded surface available. Neither wins; the tension is the work.

### Paint Systems

Wet-on-wet oil, worked while the surface is still moving. Paint Systems follows botanical structure — orchid, root, fruiting body, branch — but lets the behaviour of the medium decide where a form resolves and where it runs. Growth described by a material that is itself still moving.

### Zones

Cascades of refracted colour, built from interference rather than drawing. Zones layers iridescent striations until the surface reads as something between water, ice, and light through a prism. The palette is cool and the structure vertical; the eye falls through it rather than across it.

## Files prepared for upload

`products-upload/` — 40 JPEGs, named to match their product handle exactly,
so matching them back after upload is deterministic rather than fuzzy.

Converted from PNG at quality 92 with no chroma subsampling (saturated edges
in this work would otherwise pick up artefacts). Colour profiles preserved.

- 637 MB → 132 MB
- Largest file now 6.1 MB (Shopify rejects over 20 MB)
- Nine source PNGs were over the limit and would have failed

**Resolution note:** 12 files (all Nova) are 4.8 MP; the rest are 12 MP.
Across a 48" work that is ~56 PPI versus ~88. It shows in the zoom view.
Worth re-exporting the Nova set to match the others at some point.
